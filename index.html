FAQ

Questions

When can I download Kfanly?

Kfanly is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. Follow @kfanly.app to hear the moment it launches.

Is it free?

Yes — the Pilot and Season 1, plus Episode 1 of every season, are free. Kfanly Pro unlocks every episode and new seasons as they’re released, with a 7-day free trial.

I can’t read Hangul yet. Is that okay?

Absolutely. The Pilot season teaches Hangul from zero in about 30 minutes, with audio for every letter.

Do I need an account?

No. Kfanly works without signing up, and your learning data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone and choose Kfanly.

Need help?

We usually reply within 1–2 business days.
